# Music bed

Drop royalty-free / CC0 `.mp3` tracks in this folder. When at least one is
present, the stitcher mixes a randomly chosen track under the voiceover at low
volume (12%). With no tracks here, clips ship with voiceover only.

Good sources: pixabay.com/music, freepd.com, incompetech.com (check each
track's license before commercial use).
